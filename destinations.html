01

Nepal

Home to the world's highest mountains, ancient traditions, diverse landscapes and welcoming communities, Nepal offers experiences that stay with you long after your journey ends.

01 / NEPAL

Kathmandu

Discover Nepal's cultural heart, where ancient temples, historic squares, lively streets and timeless traditions come together in a city full of character.

02 / NEPAL

Pokhara

A peaceful lakeside city surrounded by Himalayan views, beautiful scenery and adventure. Pokhara is the perfect place to unwind or begin a mountain journey.

03 / NEPAL

Chitwan

Explore lush jungles, rich wildlife and the natural beauty of the Terai. Chitwan offers a memorable wildlife experience and a different side of Nepal.

04 / NEPAL

Mustang

Journey through dramatic desert-like landscapes, ancient monasteries and traditional villages in a region shaped by Himalayan culture and history.

05 / NEPAL

Everest Base Camp

Follow legendary trails through Sherpa villages, mountain valleys and breathtaking scenery on an unforgettable journey towards Everest Base Camp.

06 / NEPAL

Annapurna Base Camp

Walk through charming villages, forests and alpine landscapes as the trail leads into the breathtaking Annapurna mountain sanctuary.

07 / NEPAL

Langtang Valley

Experience mountain trails, alpine forests, glaciers and Tamang culture in a beautiful valley close to the Kathmandu region.

09 / NEPAL

Gosaikunda

Discover the sacred alpine lake of Gosaikunda, surrounded by rugged Himalayan mountains and peaceful high-altitude landscapes.

02

Bhutan

Discover a peaceful Himalayan kingdom where mountain landscapes, monasteries, traditional architecture and spiritual heritage create a truly unique experience.

01 / BHUTAN

Tiger's Nest

Explore scenic valleys, traditional architecture and the iconic Tiger's Nest Monastery perched dramatically on a mountainside.

02 / BHUTAN

Punakha

Enjoy peaceful river valleys, beautiful dzongs, lush surroundings and the cultural charm of Bhutan's historic former capital.

03

Tibet

Journey through the vast landscapes of the Tibetan plateau, a land of sacred mountains, spiritual traditions and remarkable natural beauty.

01 / TIBET

Kailash & Mansarovar

Experience a sacred journey to Mount Kailash and Lake Mansarovar, surrounded by the vast and extraordinary landscapes of the Tibetan plateau.
